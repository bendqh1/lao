Laotians drop many Thai classifiers but still normally use some major Thai classifiers.

1. ຄົນ (คน) – people
2. ໂຕ (ตัว) – animals and general objetcs (and also sizing in Lao as in tuanoy tuahyai) 
3. ຈອກ – cups and drinking glasses
4. ຖ້ວຍ (ถ้วย) – small bowls
5. ຂວດ (ขวด) – bottles
6. ໃບ (ใบ) – leaves, documents, cups
7. ໜ່ວຍ (หน่วย) – round fruits and objects
8. ດອກ (ดอก) – flowers
9. ຄູ່ (คู่) – pairs, shoes
10. ຄັນ (คัน) – vehicles, umbrellas
11. ຫຼັງ (หลัง) – houses and buildings
12. ເຫຼັ້ມ (เล่ม) – books and notebooks
13. ມ້ວນ (ม้วน) – rolls, toilet paper and tissue rolls
14. ກ່ອງ (กล่อง) – boxes
15. ແຜ່ນ (แผ่น) – sheets, paper, flat objects
16. ຜືນ (ผืน) – cloth, blankets, mats
17. ເສັ້ນ (เส้น) – noodles, roads, lines
18. ລຳ (ลำ) – boats, bamboo stalks
19. ເຄື່ອງ (เครื่อง) – machines and appliances
20. ອັນ (อัน) – general objects
